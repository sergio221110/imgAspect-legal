imgAspect Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

imgAspect is a local browser extension for Edge, Firefox, and Chrome. It measures design screenshots you choose to open.

Data we do not collect

  • No accounts, analytics, ads, or crash reports.
  • Images are read only on your device. They are not uploaded to a server by imgAspect.
  • The extension does not request host permissions and does not read other websites.

Data stored on your device

Theme, language, daily export count, and an optional Pro license key are stored with chrome.storage.local (extension) or localStorage (website). You can remove them by clearing site/extension data.

Payments

If you buy Pro, checkout is handled by Creem, Paddle, or Afdian. Those providers process payment details under their own policies. imgAspect only stores the license key you paste back into the app.

Refunds

One-time Pro licenses may be refunded within 7 days if the key has not been widely shared. Contact the support email on the store listing.

Contact

Use the developer email on the Microsoft Edge / Firefox store listing. See also the terms of sale.
